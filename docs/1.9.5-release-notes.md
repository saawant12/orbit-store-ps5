# Orbit Store 1.9.5 (Beta)

> **🎉 OrbitNet is coming soon: online multiplayer for jailbroken PS5s.** From the developer of Orbit. Follow along at [getorbitnet.com](https://getorbitnet.com).

**Big update: Orbit gets Kraken support and can now also get a game with all its DLC in one go.**

- **Get everything.** On a game’s page, **Get everything** lists the game and every DLC Orbit has for it. Select **Download all**, and Orbit adds them to Downloads one after another and installs them in order: the game first, then its DLC. It works in every look of the TV app, in the browser version and in Orbit Zero.
- **Queue games before you have room to unpack them.** ZIP, 7z and RAR downloads now only need room for the download itself. Orbit checks the room to unpack a game just before it unpacks it. If there isn’t enough, the download pauses and keeps the archive; free up space and select **Resume**.
- **Kraken Mount support.** With Kraken Mount running, Orbit hands your downloaded PS5 games to it, shows its progress in Downloads, and Library lists the games it has added.
- **343 more games and 36 more DLC.** The catalogue now has 3,057 games (1,323 PS5 and 1,734 PS4) and 667 DLC.
- **Fixes.** The browser version no longer loses its connection (“Connection lost”) while downloading or browsing. A download that failed before it started, for example when TorBox couldn’t prepare the file, can now be downloaded again. In the TV app, moving to Search no longer opens the keyboard and types a letter; press Cross to open it.

## Orbit Zero · Mac, Windows and Linux

- **Get everything,** as on the PS5, for games your PS5 downloads itself.
- **Download space fixes.** Zero no longer says there isn’t enough space when there is, and the room to unpack a game is checked just before it unpacks. Turn off **Extract archives after downloading** in **Settings → Storage & cleanup** to keep ZIP, 7z and RAR downloads packed.
- **Big transfers to your PS5 resume reliably.** When the connection drops, Zero carries on by itself, and resuming no longer fails while your PS5 checks what it already has. Game folders with many small files also send faster.

## Install Orbit Zero on your computer

Choose the package matching your computer from **Assets**: macOS DMG, Windows installer or ZIP, or Ubuntu/Debian DEB. Linux TAR.GZ packages are also available. Both ARM64 and x64 builds are included; Linux DEB names use **amd64** for Intel/AMD computers. Quit the older app before installing the new version. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/blob/main/guides/orbit-zero-installation.md) for setup and operating-system permissions.

## Install the native TV app (FFPKG)

Download `PPSA99177.ffpkg` from **Assets**, verify its `.sha256`, and copy it to `/data/homebrew/` on your PS5. Let ShadowMountPlus register it, then open **Orbit Store** from the Games row. Native app **1.13.5** includes the matching Orbit Store **1.9.5** service.

If an older Orbit service is running, select **Stop Orbit to restart** in its browser interface before opening the updated app so the new service can start. Pairing, settings and your queue remain saved. This control is under **App settings → Updates & startup** in this release; older releases may call the section **Update / reinstall** or **Updates**. [Native installation and update guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/blob/main/guides/getting-started.md#update-the-tv-app).

## Use or update the browser version (ELF)

Download `orbit_store.elf` and its `.sha256` from **Assets**, verify the checksum, then run the ELF through Payload Manager or your ELF loader. Open the Orbit shortcut in the Media tab, or `http://<ps5-ip>:34177/` on another device on the same local network.

To replace a running older version, select **Stop Orbit to restart**, then launch the new ELF. Orbit Zero can upload Orbit under **Settings → Updates & startup → Start Orbit on PS5**. It checks the official feed for a newer compatible service; its included offline fallback is PS5 service **1.9.5**.

On Orbit 1.5.0 or later, **Update Orbit** updates the TV app and the service together. In the native app from 1.8.2 on, select **Close and reopen** when asked; the reopened app starts its bundled service. Browser-only users can restart automatically with an active ELF loader on port 9021, or launch the updated ELF manually through their payload manager.

## Downloads and source

- **PS5:** `PPSA99177.ffpkg` includes the native TV app and download service. Use `orbit_store.elf` to run the service and browser interface on their own.
- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** choose the `Orbit-Zero-1.9.5-*` package for your operating system and processor. These installers work with both PS5 and PS4. They include the PS5 1.9.5 service, whose source is in this release’s source bundle, and elfldr a481740, whose source and licences are in the [PS4 0.0.3 source bundle](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/releases/tag/v0.0.3).
- **Source code:** `orbit-store-1.9.5-source-bundle.zip` includes the Store source code, dependencies, licence notices and build instructions.
- **Checksums:** use `SHA256SUMS` to check your download, or run `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256` for the ELF or FFPKG.
