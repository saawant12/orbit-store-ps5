# Orbit Store 1.8.2 (Beta)

**Smoother updates, better download choices and a growing catalogue.**

- **Simpler native updates.** From this version on, updating Orbit from the TV app downloads the app once when it already includes the newest download service. Select **Close and reopen** when it finishes. The reopened app starts its new download service by itself, and your downloads continue.
- **Game pages start on the right option.** Opening a game from filtered Browse results selects the download option that matches your filters. Otherwise Orbit selects the source and format you downloaded last, then an option that downloads without a verification page.

- **The catalogue now has 2,158 games and 5,045 download options**, plus **626 add-ons with 628 options across 192 base games**.

> **Orbit stays free.** [Orbit Store](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5), [Orbit Zero](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5#orbit-zero-for-mac-windows-and-linux), [Orbit Play](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-play-ps5) and our upcoming apps are free and will stay free.

We’re also shipping improvements and bug fixes across our apps:

## Orbit Store · Native PS5 app (FFPKG) and browser (ELF)

- **Easier to read time left.** Downloads longer than an hour show hours and minutes, and long retry waits show minutes.
- **Clear finished copies and moves.** Library results such as “Copied … · Complete” can now be dismissed.
- **Downloads start during copies and transfers.** Fixed a bug that kept new and resumed downloads waiting while a game was being copied or moved, or while Orbit Zero was sending a file. These operations reserve their remaining space and protect files in use.
- **Messages clear after a retry.** Fixed a bug where a message such as “The download provider is temporarily unavailable” stayed on screen after the download had already resumed.
- **Restarts keep the newest version.** Fixed a restart that could start an older saved copy of Orbit.
- **TV app installs try again.** Installing or updating the TV app waits longer for GitHub and tries once more before showing an error.
- **Failed transfers stop blocking.** A transfer from Orbit Zero that failed before sending anything no longer keeps Library copies and Files actions waiting. Orbit releases it after 10 minutes.

## Orbit Zero · Mac, Windows and Linux

- **Easier to read time left.** Transfers longer than an hour show hours and minutes.
- **Start Orbit on PS5 without a false error.** Fixed **Console → Start Orbit on PS5** reporting “Payload loader stopped responding.” after Orbit had already started.

## Install Orbit Zero on your computer

Choose the package matching your computer from **Assets**: macOS DMG, Windows installer or ZIP, or Ubuntu/Debian DEB. Linux TAR.GZ packages are also available. Both ARM64 and x64 builds are included; Linux DEB names use **amd64** for Intel/AMD computers. Quit the older app before installing the new version. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/blob/main/guides/orbit-zero-installation.md) for setup and operating-system permissions.

## Install the native TV app (FFPKG)

Download `PPSA99177.ffpkg` from **Assets**, verify its `.sha256`, and copy it to `/data/homebrew/` on your PS5. Let ShadowMountPlus register it, then open **Orbit Store** from the Games row. Native app **1.12.2** includes the matching Orbit Store **1.8.2** service.

If an older Orbit service is running, select **Stop Orbit to restart** in its browser interface before opening the updated app so the new service can start. Pairing, settings and your queue remain saved. This control is under **App settings → Updates & startup** in this release; older releases may call the section **Update / reinstall** or **Updates**. [Native installation and update guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/blob/main/guides/getting-started.md#update-the-tv-app).

## Use or update the browser version (ELF)

Download `orbit_store.elf` and its `.sha256` from **Assets**, verify the checksum, then run the ELF through Payload Manager or your ELF loader. Open the Orbit shortcut in the Media tab, or `http://<ps5-ip>:34177/` on another device on the same local network.

To replace a running older version, select **Stop Orbit to restart**, then launch the new ELF. Orbit Zero also includes the matching ELF under **Console → Start Orbit on PS5**, so you can upload it from your computer without downloading the ELF separately.

On Orbit 1.5.0 or later, **Update Orbit** updates the TV app and the service together. In the native app from 1.8.2 on, select **Close and reopen** when asked; the reopened app starts its bundled service. Browser-only users can restart automatically with an active ELF loader on port 9021, or launch the updated ELF manually through their payload manager.

## Downloads and source

- `orbit_store.elf` is the PS5 service and browser interface; `PPSA99177.ffpkg` is the native TV app with the same service included.
- `Orbit-Zero-*` files are the desktop packages. Choose the operating system and architecture matching your computer.
- The `orbit-store-1.8.2-source-bundle.zip` contains the corresponding Store application and dependency sources, licence notices and rebuild instructions.
- `SHA256SUMS` covers the release assets. Verify a binary with `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256`, or use `SHA256SUMS` for the desktop packages.
