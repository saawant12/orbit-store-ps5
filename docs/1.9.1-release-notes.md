# Orbit Store 1.9.1 (Beta)

> **🎉 Orbit is now on PS4 too.** Get the new [Orbit Store for PS4](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4) port, with downloads and setup instructions in its own repository.

- **ZSTD comes to Orbit.** Find ZSTD games with the Format filter and see the format in download details, across the PS5 browser and native app's four layouts. Orbit unpacks the outer archive and keeps the ZSTD game assets ready for a compatible loader and runtime.
- **More download choices.** Orbit now has **2,714 games with 5,679 download options**, plus **631 add-ons with 633 options across 197 base games**.

We’re also shipping improvements and bug fixes across our apps:

## Orbit Store · Native PS5 app (FFPKG) and browser (ELF)

- **More reliable installation and app updates.** Installation history no longer prevents new installs when it fills up. The native app installer also handles an already-registered Orbit app more reliably.

## Orbit Zero · Mac, Windows and Linux

- **Let your PS5 handle the download.** Set **Settings → Downloads → Download preference** to **Prefer PS5**. With Orbit Zero 1.9.1, Vikingfile and AkiraBox verification opens on your computer. Zero sends the ready link to Orbit on your PS5, which checks it and downloads the game directly. If the link cannot be used there, Zero can download and transfer the files instead.
- **One app for PS5 and PS4.** Pair either console and keep a separate profile for its connection, games and downloads. Switch between consoles without pairing again, once unfinished console transfers are finished or cancelled.

The Zero installers are identical to those on the [Orbit for PS4 0.0.2 release](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/releases/tag/v0.0.2). They include the PS5 1.9.0 service as an offline fallback; update the running PS5 service to **1.9.1** for the captured-link handoff. Zero obtains newer compatible services from the official update feed when online.

## Install Orbit Zero on your computer

Choose the package matching your computer from **Assets**: macOS DMG, Windows installer or ZIP, or Ubuntu/Debian DEB. Linux TAR.GZ packages are also available. Both ARM64 and x64 builds are included; Linux DEB names use **amd64** for Intel/AMD computers. Quit the older app before installing the new version. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/blob/main/guides/orbit-zero-installation.md) for setup and operating-system permissions.

## Install the native TV app (FFPKG)

Download `PPSA99177.ffpkg` from **Assets**, verify its `.sha256`, and copy it to `/data/homebrew/` on your PS5. Let ShadowMountPlus register it, then open **Orbit Store** from the Games row. Native app **1.13.1** includes the matching Orbit Store **1.9.1** service.

If an older Orbit service is running, select **Stop Orbit to restart** in its browser interface before opening the updated app so the new service can start. Pairing, settings and your queue remain saved. This control is under **App settings → Updates & startup** in this release; older releases may call the section **Update / reinstall** or **Updates**. [Native installation and update guide](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/blob/main/guides/getting-started.md#update-the-tv-app).

## Use or update the browser version (ELF)

Download `orbit_store.elf` and its `.sha256` from **Assets**, verify the checksum, then run the ELF through Payload Manager or your ELF loader. Open the Orbit shortcut in the Media tab, or `http://<ps5-ip>:34177/` on another device on the same local network.

To replace a running older version, select **Stop Orbit to restart**, then launch the new ELF. Orbit Zero can upload Orbit under **Settings → Updates & startup → Start Orbit on PS5**. It checks the official feed for a newer compatible service; its included offline fallback is PS5 service **1.9.0**. To start 1.9.1 without fetching the update feed, choose its ELF under **Advanced → Choose another ELF**.

On Orbit 1.5.0 or later, **Update Orbit** updates the TV app and the service together. In the native app from 1.8.2 on, select **Close and reopen** when asked; the reopened app starts its bundled service. Browser-only users can restart automatically with an active ELF loader on port 9021, or launch the updated ELF manually through their payload manager.

## Downloads and source

- **PS5:** `PPSA99177.ffpkg` includes the native TV app and download service. Use `orbit_store.elf` to run the service and browser interface on their own.
- **Mac, Windows and Linux:** choose the `Orbit-Zero-1.9.1-*` package for your operating system and processor. These shared PS5/PS4 installers include the PS5 1.9.0 service; its source bundle is on the [1.9.0 release](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps5/releases/tag/v1.9.0). The bundled PS4 elfldr 0.7 source and licences are included in the [PS4 0.0.2 source bundle](https://github.com/saawant12/orbit-store-ps4/releases/tag/v0.0.2).
- **Source code:** `orbit-store-1.9.1-source-bundle.zip` includes the Store source code, dependencies, licence notices and build instructions.
- **Checksums:** use `SHA256SUMS` to check your download, or run `shasum -a 256 -c <filename>.sha256` for the ELF or FFPKG.
